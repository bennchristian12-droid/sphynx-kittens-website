Our Story

SphynxHaven was founded out of a love for this remarkable, affectionate breed. What began as a small home cattery has grown into a trusted source for healthy, well-socialized Sphynx kittens.

Every kitten is raised underfoot in our home, handled daily from birth, and vet-checked before going to their new family. We're with you every step of the way — from application to lifelong support.
